# Structural visualization and motif superimposition

This folder contains the two PyMOL sessions used for the manuscript structural
figures. The sessions preserve the loaded coordinate objects, named residue
selections, superpositions, representations, colors, and saved views used to
prepare the panels.

## Manuscript figure mapping

| PyMOL session | Manuscript output | Role |
|---|---|---|
| [`superfamily_logo_positions_overall_look.pse`](superfamily_logo_positions_overall_look.pse) | **Figure 3b** | Maps the 36 positions prioritized by the superfamily conservation analysis onto the `2rh1` receptor structure. The session contains the named selection `logo_residues`. Figure 3a and Figure 3c are generated by the conservation/scatter and sequence-logo workflow, not by this PyMOL session. |
| [`motifs_across_families.pse`](motifs_across_families.pse) | **Figure 4** | Superimposes experimental structures and AlphaFold models across the analyzed GPCR families and displays only the residue positions used to assess structural conservation of ancestral motifs. |

The corresponding manuscript sections are **Shared sequence motifs across GPCR
families** (Figure 3), **Structural Validation of Shared Motifs** (Figure 4),
and Methods: **Superimposition of Identified Motifs**.

## Purpose

Positions identified from the family/superfamily motif analysis are mapped onto representative structures. Experimental structures are used when available; AlphaFold models are used for families without a suitable experimental structure. More than one structure may be used when a single structure does not resolve every motif being compared.
