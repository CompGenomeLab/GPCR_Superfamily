Family origins by lineage: Dollo parsimony and observed presence

Columns are 20 non-overlapping lineages ordered along the tree above them. Dots show the share of each lineage's genomes carrying the family: black for presences the conservative interpretation rests on, orange for questionable presences, red for contamination or horizontal transfer. The Dollo origin (genus threshold k = 2) uses every presence; the conservative interpretation uses the black presences only. Hover a row to mark the conservative origin on the tree; hover a cell for counts. The root (LECA) is drawn as a polytomy of the major eukaryotic groups.
